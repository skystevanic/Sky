# Product checklist: before sending a link

## 1. Instant "no" (skip it)
- It goes on the skin, in the body or in the mouth: creams, oils, sticks, lashes, supplements.
- It plugs in, charges or heats up: masks, belts, massagers, LED devices.
- It is sold with a health promise: "relieves sinusitis", "endometriosis", "lifting", "cures".
- It is for babies or children's sleep.
- It is big or heavy (over about 3 kg or 60 cm long).
- It copies a famous brand's design (like the Tarte-style compact).

## 2. The 2-minute checks (do these yourself)
1. **Google Lens:** screenshot the product photo and search it with the camera icon in the Google app.
   If Temu, AliExpress, Shein or Walmart appear straight away, skip it.
2. **Amazon:** search the product on **amazon.de** and **amazon.nl** (also .es, .fr).
   If the same product costs under €15, skip it.
3. **Big shops:** search **leroymerlin.es**, **hornbach.de**, **decathlon.es** and **action.com**
   (plus **kmart.com.au** for gadgets). If it's there, skip it.
4. **Facebook Ad Library** (facebook.com/ads/library, search the product words):
   - Good: **one brand** running ads again and again **for 3+ months**.
   - Bad: dozens of brands, most started in the last few weeks (a swarm).
   - Not proof yet: one brand whose ads are all only days or weeks old.

## 3. What a good first product looks like
- An everyday annoyance solved **without a health claim**.
- Categories: home, kitchen, garden, car, cleaning, organisers, pet **accessories**
  (not pet food or care), hobby and sport gear (golf works well).
- **Small and light**: cheap to ship and return.
- Shows its result in **a few seconds of video**.
- Sells for about **€30-80**, and costs no more than about a quarter of that to buy.
- Bonus: **refills** or consumables, so customers come back.

## 4. What to send
- A link to the **brand's own product page** (not Amazon, AliExpress or Temu).
- If it's a collection page, say which product you mean.
- You can sell anywhere: the check covers 15 countries
  (AU, US, UK, CA, DE, NZ, NL, FR, ES, IT, BE, AT, SE, PL, IE)
  and shows which ones are still open.

## Lessons from the first 20 products
- Most products fail. That's normal: one good one is the goal.
- The best leads all had **one brand advertising alone for months**.
- A product crowded in English can still be open in another language or market
  (Benistie sells a common toilet brush alone in Hebrew).
