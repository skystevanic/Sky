# Test plan: magnetic tennis swing trainer (Netherlands + Belgium)

**Goal:** find out with about €300 whether Dutch-speaking tennis and padel players buy it.

## The numbers
| | |
|---|---|
| Your selling price | **€59.95** (€99.95 for 2: "one for you, one for your partner") |

| Good result in the test | 1 sale for every €30 of ads or less |

## What I found (Oct 19)
- **Netherlands/Belgium Google Shopping:** no one sells this exact snap stick yet. Only other trainer types appear
  (Dragonet Swing €148, Pure2Improve €10-26, Mookie €33-40).
  The closest is a "badminton swing trainer with magnetic resistance" on bol.com for €22.99. Check that it isn't the same stick.
- **Amazon:** not in the top 20 in NL or BE. Germany and the UK have copies from €13-46, so **don't sell in Germany**.
- **Swng (the US brand):** 101 ads since 3 September, almost alone.

## Step 1: Supplier (this week)
Open these two AliExpress listings (AliExpress blocks me, so you need to check them):
1. https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005010023766224.html: "Adjustable Tennis Swing Trainer", magnetic, sound, 4 levels
2. https://www.aliexpress.com/item/1005011650693027.html: "Magnetic Tennis Swing Trainer 26.5 cm"

Check each one for:
- **Price with shipping to the Netherlands.** Aim for under €15.
- **Delivery time.** Choose "AliExpress Choice" or 7-12 days if you can.
- **Orders and reviews.** 100+ orders and 4.5+ stars is best.
- **Photos from buyers:** does it look like the Swng stick?

**Order 2 samples** (about €30) to your home in Spain, to check quality and **film your own videos**.

## Step 2: Shop (while samples travel)
- **Platform:** Shopify, free trial, then about €30-40 a month. Use a domain with your own brand name, not "Swng".
- **Language:** the whole shop in **Dutch** (Flemish Belgium speaks Dutch too).
- **Payments:** turn on **iDEAL** (Netherlands) and **Bancontact** (Belgium). Many Dutch buyers won't buy without iDEAL.
- **Pages needed:** product page, shipping (honest delivery time), 14-day returns, contact, terms, privacy.
- **Own photos only.** Never use Swng's photos or videos (that's copyright).

## Step 3: Videos (when samples arrive)
Film 3-5 short vertical videos (10-20 seconds), each with a different hook:
1. "Hoor je die klik? Dan was je slag goed." (the snap sound, close up)
2. Padel player at home: "10 minuten per dag, betere slag op de baan."
3. Gift angle: "Het perfecte cadeau voor een tennisser" (**Sinterklaas is 5 Dec**, then Christmas)
4. Before and after: a messy swing vs a clean swing with the click.

## Step 4: Ads (7-day test)
- **Where:** Meta (Facebook + Instagram), Netherlands + Flemish Belgium.
- **Audience:** interests tennis, padel, KNLTB; age 25-60; or broad targeting with good videos.
- **Budget:** €20-30 a day for 7 days, about **€150-200**.
- **Rules:**
  - Spent €40 with no "add to cart": change the video.
  - Spent €100 with no sale: change the hook or price once.
  - Spent €150-200 with no sale: **stop**. You've learned it cheaply.
  - 1 sale per €30 or less: raise the budget 20-30% every 2-3 days.

## Timing
- **Order the samples now.** Ads should be running by about **10 Nov** for Sinterklaas and Christmas gifts.
- Last day to promise delivery before 5 Dec is about **20 Nov** (with 10-14 day shipping).

## Before you sell (paperwork, Spain)
- You must be registered as **autónomo** (or a company) to sell regularly. Ask a gestor (about €40-60 a month).
- EU product safety rule (GPSR): your name, address and a short safety note must be on the product page and packaging.
- Keep all supplier invoices.
